Beginner guide

Start your family tree with the stories you already know.

A calm, practical beginning is enough. You do not need every document before you start.

  1. Start with yourself, parents, and grandparents. Record full names, nicknames, dates, and places exactly as your family remembers them.
  2. Talk with a relative and ask open questions: Who was the oldest person you knew? Where did the family live? What stories were told at holidays?
  3. For each detail, write down who shared it and when. Family memory is a starting point; records help you confirm it.
  4. Pick one person and one question, such as a birth place or parent name. Search one record collection at a time, then save the result and its source.
