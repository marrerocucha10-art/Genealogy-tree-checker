Research guidance

Every family question can lead to a new discovery.

Start with one person, one place, or one event. These trusted resources help you move forward with confidence.

01

Choose one person

Write down the name, a date range, and the place you associate with them.

02

Ask one clear question

Try: “Who were her parents?” or “Where did he live in 1920?”

03

Save what you find

Keep the source, record date, and a note about why it belongs in your tree.
