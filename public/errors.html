Review and resolve family-tree errors in focused groups. Each item explains what may need attention and suggests a next step. Compare it with your original GEDCOM or research records, make the correction, then mark it solved. If an item cannot be resolved right away, move it to Fix Later (Pending). It will not block your progress, and you can return whenever you have more information. Your first five manual fixes are included at no charge; upgrade when you are ready to correct the rest or use safe automatic fixes.

Where corrections are saved: Your original uploaded GEDCOM is never overwritten. Confirmed duplicate merges and safe automatic fixes update the working family-tree copy saved in this browser.
