Intermediate guide

Build stronger evidence for every family connection.

Move from collecting names to comparing evidence. The goal is a tree you can explain and revisit.

  1. Create a simple timeline for one person: birth, census appearances, marriage, children, moves, and death. Gaps in the timeline show your best next question.
  2. Compare names, ages, relatives, and places across at least two records. One matching fact is a clue; several matching facts form stronger evidence.
  3. When records disagree, preserve both versions. Note which source is closest to the event and which facts each source supports.
  4. Use a research log: question, place searched, record set, result, and next step. This stops repeated searches and makes your work easier to share.
