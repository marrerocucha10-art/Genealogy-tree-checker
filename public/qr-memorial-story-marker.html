How it works

  1. Create the memorial. Add the person’s story and choose what photos and recordings to share.
  2. Review family access. Confirm the memorial page and decide who can view it before publishing.
  3. Generate the permanent QR link. The QR code points to the approved memorial page.
  4. Engrave and place the marker. Use a durable material and follow the memorial location’s rules.

Privacy comes first

Memorial pages, photographs, and voice recordings will be private by default. The live media upload and access controls are the next implementation step; this product page does not publish or store memorial content yet.
